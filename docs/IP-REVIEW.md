## Copyright and provenance: notices prepared, registration not filed

Indian copyright guidance distinguishes protectable expression from an unprotected business idea. Registering code does not give ownership of the general idea of renting unused parking spaces. Names and short phrases are also not ordinarily protected by copyright alone. Copyright registration can provide evidentiary value; a Git commit, GitHub repository or copyright notice is not a government registration.

Retain source history, design drafts, image-generation records, original host photo permissions and any contributor/contractor agreements. If a company is formed, have any required assignments documented rather than assuming the company automatically owns personal or contractor work. The applicable authorship and protectability of AI-assisted code and logo output require separate assessment; no exclusive copyright eligibility is certified here.

The approved logo was generated with AI during development. It is not a stock photograph, but its appearance still needs similarity/device-mark review. A human designer can prepare a vector master and record their creative contribution after the brand is cleared. No reverse-image or complete logo-similarity clearance was performed.

## Patents: preliminary prior art found; patentability and freedom to operate unresolved

The term “pattern” in the request has been interpreted as **patent**. A patent protects an eligible claimed invention, not merely a company name or the decision to build a parking marketplace. India's Patents Act section 3(k) excludes business methods and computer programs per se. Technical inventions need analysis under the current law and computer-related-invention guidance; it would be inaccurate to declare every software invention unpatentable.
